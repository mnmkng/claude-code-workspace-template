# Quality assurance context

Quality assurance covers inbound stock quality and supplier relations: what
arrives from the mills and converters, whether it meets the spec we sold, and
what happens with the supplier when it does not. It is not a manufacturing
function - we do not make paper - and it is not product testing.

Its two outputs that other departments depend on: the inspection result that
releases stock for sale, and the rejection record that accounts payable needs
before paying a supplier invoice.
