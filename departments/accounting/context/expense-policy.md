# Expense policy

What the company reimburses, up to what limit, who approves it, and when the
money arrives. Applies to every employee at both branches and corporate.

The `expense-report-check` skill checks a submitted report against this file.
Limits are as of August 2026.

## Categories and limits

| Category | Limit | Receipt required | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Client meal | $60 per person | Always | Name the account ID and who attended. Alcohol is not reimbursed |
| Employee meal, travelling | $45 per day | Over $25 | Only on an overnight trip or a day trip over 100 miles |
| Mileage, own vehicle | Federal standard rate per mile | Log required, not a receipt | Between client sites and the branch. Home to branch is commuting and is not reimbursed |
| Fuel, company vehicle | Actual | Always | Company fuel card preferred; personal payment is an exception |
| Parking and tolls | $30 per day | Over $10 | |
| Lodging | $180 per night | Always | Book through the company travel arrangement where one exists |
| Air or rail travel | Economy or coach only | Always | Booked at least 14 days ahead unless there is a written reason |
| Client entertainment | $100 per person | Always | Prior written approval from the regional manager. Never for a public-sector buyer |
| Office supplies bought locally | $75 per month | Over $25 | Only when the warehouse cannot supply it; note why |
| Mobile phone | $50 per month | Not required | Flat allowance for roles with a client-facing phone number |
| Professional membership or certification | $500 per year | Always | Approved in advance by the department head |
| Training and conferences | $2,000 per year | Always | Approved in advance by the department head and human resources |
| Safety equipment and work footwear | $200 per year | Always | Warehouse staff. Not counted against any other limit |

Anything not on this table needs written approval from the Chief Financial
Officer **before** the money is spent.

## Never reimbursed

- Alcohol, in any category, including on a client meal.
- Traffic fines, parking tickets, and towing.
- Travel upgrades, lounge access, and seat selection fees.
- Personal entertainment, gifts to employees, and personal grooming.
- Home to branch commuting, in any vehicle.
- A client gift over $25, which is a separate policy and goes through the
  regional manager.
- Anything for a public-sector buyer beyond a meal at the standard limit.
  Counties and school districts have gift and hospitality rules that are theirs,
  not ours, and breaking one costs the account and the bid.

## Approval chain

| Report total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $250 | Direct manager |
| $251 to $1,000 | Department head |
| $1,001 to $5,000 | Regional manager, or the department head at corporate |
| Above $5,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Plus, regardless of total:

- **Any client entertainment line** needs the regional manager, whatever the
  total, and prior written approval.
- **Any line with no receipt where one is required** needs the department head,
  whatever the total.
- **Nobody approves their own report.** A manager's report goes to their own
  manager. A report approved by its submitter is returned unpaid.

## Submission and reimbursement

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Submit | By the 5th of the month, for the previous month |
| Approve | Within 3 business days of submission |
| Branch accounting review | Days 6 to 10 |
| Reimbursement | With the following month's payroll run |

A report submitted after the 5th moves to the next cycle. There is no
out-of-cycle payment except for a documented hardship, approved by the Chief
Financial Officer.

Expenses older than **90 days** are not reimbursed. The limit exists because a
receipt that old cannot be reconciled against a closed month.

## Receipts

- An itemized receipt, not a card slip. A card slip shows a total and proves
  nothing about what was bought - it is the most common reason a report comes
  back.
- Legible, whole, and showing the merchant, date, and items.
- A photograph is fine. Attach it to the line, not to the report as a whole.
- **Lost receipt:** a written statement with the date, merchant, amount, and
  business purpose, approved by the department head. Twice a year per person,
  not as routine.

## Client meals and the account ID

Every client meal and entertainment line names the **account ID**, not the
client. Account IDs look like `DM-SCR-0412`. Names of individual client staff do
not go on an expense line at all.

This is the same rule the sales department works under, for the same reason:
expense reports are retained for seven years and are read by people with no
relationship to the account.

## Common reasons a report is returned

In order of frequency (as of Q2 2026), against a return rate of 18%:

1. A card slip instead of an itemized receipt.
2. A client meal over $60 per person, with no prior approval.
3. A missing account ID on a client meal.
4. Alcohol on an itemized restaurant receipt, not split out.
5. Home to branch mileage claimed as a client visit.
6. Submitted after the 5th, so held to the next cycle.
