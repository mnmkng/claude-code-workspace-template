# Scranton sales context

Territory, cadence, and local practice for the Scranton branch sales team. The
process, pricing, approval chain, and metrics are the department's and are not
restated here.

## Team structure

| Role | Count | Reports to |
|------|-------|------------|
| Regional Manager | 1 | Vice President, Northeast Sales |
| Assistant to the Regional Manager | 1 | Regional Manager |
| Sales Representative | 6 | Regional Manager |

Counts are as of August 2026.

The Assistant to the Regional Manager carries a full book of accounts in
addition to the coordination role, and holds discount authority one step above a
representative. The title is written in full; it is not "Assistant Regional
Manager".

## Territory

Lackawanna County and the surrounding delivery radius: roughly 45 minutes'
drive from the Scranton warehouse. Territories are assigned by geography, not by
industry, so a representative's accounts sit on one or two delivery routes.

| Area | Notes |
|---|---|
| Scranton city and inner ring | Densest small-business territory; most call volume |
| Lackawanna County offices and districts | Public segment; runs on the bid calendar, not on call activity |
| Outer townships | Longer drives; visits are batched into one day a week |
| Hospital systems in the radius | Named accounts, held at the regional manager's level |

Anything outside the radius is a third-party carrier delivery, quoted with the
freight passed through, and is not prospected into.

## Cadence

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Daily, 9:00-11:00 a.m. ET | Call block. Prospecting and reactivation calls, no internal meetings scheduled into it |
| Monday, 11:30 a.m. ET | Branch pipeline stand-up, 30 minutes, pipeline changes only |
| Wednesday | Visit day for the outer townships; the drives are batched |
| Friday, end of day | CRM hygiene: activity logged, stages current, closed-lost reasons filled in |
| Monthly, first week | Territory and route review with the warehouse manager |
| Quarterly | Account reviews on accounts over $25K a year, with the regional manager |

The call block is the branch's one protected hour and the activity most likely
to be skipped. New-account performance tracks it directly.

## Local practice

- **Trial deliveries are offered on every small-business deal.** Scranton
  warehouses roughly 600 stock-keeping units, so a trial can usually go out next
  day, and it is the single most effective closing move available here.
- **Coordinate with the warehouse before promising an expedited delivery.**
  Same-day capacity is one constraint shared across all seven books of accounts
  on this team, not a per-representative allowance.
- **The bid calendar is branch-level.** County and district bid dates are
  tracked by the regional manager. A representative who finds a bid date puts it
  on that calendar the same day.
- **Referrals are worked the week they arrive.** Referral is the
  highest-converting channel we have, and it decays fast.
- **Utica coverage.** Scranton does not prospect or quote inside the Utica
  radius. An inbound lead from there is handed to the Utica regional manager the
  same day, with the account created in the CRM first.
